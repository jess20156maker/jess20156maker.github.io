# Mulai Villa social goals

Status: proposed targets. Recalibrate after four weeks of real numbers (see "Baseline").

## What social is for

One villa has roughly 365 nights a year to sell. It does not need a large audience. It
needs a small, right one: people who can afford the villa, are planning a trip to
Indonesia, and trust us enough to message directly instead of booking through an OTA.

So the order of priority is:

1. **Direct enquiries** (WhatsApp messages and website enquiries that came from Instagram).
2. **Saves and shares** (the signal that someone is planning a trip or showing a partner).
3. **Followers** (a useful proxy for trust, and the audience for future offers).

## 12-month targets (Instagram, assuming a start near zero)

| Milestone | Followers | Enquiries from Instagram / month | Direct bookings from Instagram |
|---|---|---|---|
| Month 1 (launch) | 300 | 1-2 | 0 |
| Month 3 | 1,000 | 4 | 1 in the quarter |
| Month 6 | 2,500 | 8 | 1 per month |
| Month 12 | 5,000 | 12-15 | 2 per month |

If the account already has followers, keep the same shape: about +25% a month for the
first three months, then +10 to 15% a month. Travel and hospitality accounts average
closer to 1 to 2% a month, so these targets assume consistent Reels, creator stays and
a small ad budget, not posting alone.

5,000 real followers in the right markets is worth more than 50,000 from giveaways.
Never buy followers, run follow-for-follow, or use engagement pods: they wreck reach
and make the numbers useless.

## Weekly quality targets

| Metric | Target | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Feed posts published | 4 (2 Reels, 1 carousel, 1 photo) | Consistency is the main growth lever |
| Stories | 3+ days a week | Keeps existing followers warm |
| Engagement rate (interactions / followers) | 4%+ while under 5k followers | Small-account norm is about 3 to 5% |
| Saves + shares per feed post | 3% of reach | Planning intent |
| Reels reach from non-followers | 60%+ | Reels are how small accounts get found |
| Profile visits to link clicks | 5%+ | Is the bio and link doing its job |
| Reply time to DMs and comments | Under 12 hours | A DM is a booking enquiry in disguise |

## Audience quality target

By month 6, at least 60% of followers in the source markets: Australia, UK,
Netherlands, Germany, Singapore. Check in Instagram Insights monthly. If the audience
drifts toward markets that will never book, change the hashtags, creators and ad targeting.

## Supporting goals

- **Guest content:** one piece of guest photo or video permission per stay.
- **Creators:** one hosted creator stay per quarter (10k to 100k followers, audience in a
  source market, with agreed deliverables).
- **Owned list:** every enquirer is added to the email/WhatsApp list with consent. Instagram
  is rented land; the list is the asset.
- **Paid:** once three posts have proven themselves organically, boost the best one to
  source markets. Start at about USD 5 to 10 a day and judge by cost per enquiry.
