# Mulai Villa on social: brand style

The feed should feel like the villa does at 7am: quiet, light, unhurried. Reference
points: Aman, Alila, Soho Farmhouse. Never a listing, never a travel agency.

## Voice

Personality: serene, warm, design-led, authentic, understated.

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Calm and sensory: light, air, water, stillness | Hyped: "paradise", "bucket list", "unforgettable" |
| Specific: "coffee on the daybed at six" | Generic: "luxury amenities and stunning views" |
| A host speaking: "we", "our team", first names | A brochure, or a brand speaking in the third person |
| Confident and brief | Salesy, urgent, discount-led |
| Rooted in Lombok and Sasak culture, with respect | Using local people or culture as a backdrop |

**Caption rules**

- The first line is the hook and must make sense on its own.
- Under 600 characters. Short paragraphs. Plain words.
- One idea per post.
- End with a soft invitation, not a command: "Save this for the trip you keep meaning to
  take", "Send this to whoever you would bring", "Message us to begin."
- No more than one emoji, and usually none. No exclamation marks.
- 3 to 5 specific hashtags, kept out of the caption body.
- British/Australian spelling.
- Never state a fact about the villa that has not been confirmed.

**Signature language:** "to begin", "begin here", "slowly", "yours alone", "the house",
"our team", "we will look after the rest".

## Visual style

**Photography**

- Natural light only. Shoot in the first and last hour of the day. No flash, no HDR.
- Warm, soft, slightly matte. One consistent edit on everything (a single preset:
  lower contrast, warm whites, greens pulled toward olive, no heavy teal-and-orange).
- Space and stillness. Leave room in the frame. Empty rooms that look just left.
- People from behind, at a distance, or hands only. Real guests and team, never stock.
- Detail over drone. One aerial a month at most.
- No text on photos, no borders, no stickers on feed posts.

**Video**

- Slow, steady clips of 2 to 3 seconds. Tripod or very gentle movement.
- Natural sound first: water, birds, wind, a kitchen. Quiet instrumental if needed.
  No trending audio that will date the post, no voiceover filters.
- Text on screen: one line, first frame only, small.

**Colour palette (proposed, to confirm against the villa's real materials)**

| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Limewash | `#F4EFE6` | Backgrounds, text slides |
| Sand | `#D9C7AD` | Secondary backgrounds |
| Teak | `#8A6A4B` | Accents, lines |
| Palm | `#5C6B55` | Accent, sparingly |
| Volcanic | `#2B2A28` | Text |

These are a starting proposal drawn from the "light, air, water, stone" direction. If a
logo or palette already exists, it replaces this table.

**Typography (proposed):** an elegant serif for titles (Cormorant Garamond or similar)
with a quiet sans for small text (Inter or similar). Titles in sentence case, generous
spacing, never bold caps.

**Text slides and Reel covers:** Limewash background, Volcanic serif title, nothing
else. Every Reel gets a cover chosen so the grid stays calm.

**Grid rhythm:** alternate wide and close, light and shade. Never two text slides or
two pool shots side by side. Check the next six posts together before approving a week.

## Post styles (the recurring series)

Each post belongs to a series. Series make the account recognisable and make weekly
planning fast.

| Series | Pillar | Format | What it is |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mulai mornings** | Villa | Reel | 10 to 15 seconds of the villa waking up. Natural sound. The signature series. |
| **The detail** | Villa | Photo | One object, surface or corner, and the story of who made it. |
| **Room by room** | Villa | Carousel | One space, 5 to 7 frames, wide to close. |
| **Lombok, slowly** | Place | Carousel or Reel | One day, one beach, one meal. Guides worth saving. |
| **The people** | People | Photo or Reel | A team member, by first name, in their own words. |
| **From the kitchen** | People | Reel | Breakfast, a local dish, the market run. |
| **Guest book** | Guests | Photo or carousel | A guest's photo or a line from a review, with permission. |
| **Begin here** | Booking | Reel or carousel | How to book, what is included, open dates, why direct. |
| **To begin** | Ethos | Text slide or Reel | A short thought on slower travel. Rare. |

## Profile

- **Name field:** Mulai Villa | Private villa, Lombok
- **Bio (draft):** A private villa in Lombok, yours alone. / Mulai: to begin. / Book
  direct with our team below.
- **Link:** one page with WhatsApp enquiry first, then the website and availability.
- **Highlights:** The villa, Rooms, Lombok, Our team, Guest book, How to book.
  Limewash covers with a single small Teak line icon.

## Community rules

- Reply to every comment and DM within 12 hours, signed with a first name.
- Answer price questions directly in DMs and offer to check dates.
- Ask before reposting any guest content, and credit the guest.
- Team members appear only with their consent, and are named the way they prefer.
