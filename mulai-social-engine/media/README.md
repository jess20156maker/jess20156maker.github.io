# Media library

Put approved photos and videos here, one folder per week: `media/2026-W48/tue-reel.mp4`.
Then list the path in the post's `media` field (relative to `mulai-social-engine/`),
for example `"media": ["media/2026-W48/tue-reel.mp4"]`.

- Reels and stories: 9:16 vertical, 1080x1920, MP4, under 90 seconds.
- Photos and carousels: 4:5 portrait, 1080x1350, JPG.
- Large video files should not be committed to git. Keep them in a shared drive and
  copy them in before running `publish`; `media/*/` is ignored by git for that reason.

## Standing shot list (capture once, reuse for months)

Shoot these in one or two sessions, in the first and last hour of daylight.

1. Pool at dawn, midday and dusk: wide, and close on the water surface.
2. Every bedroom: wide from the doorway, plus two details each (linen, light, view).
3. Open-air living: wide, empty, then lived-in (a book, a glass, a towel).
4. Breakfast being set up and served: hands, not posed faces.
5. Each team member at work, candid, with their consent to appear.
6. Ten handmade or local details: textiles, timber, ceramics, plants, stone.
7. Arrival: the road in, the gate, the first view through the door.
8. Three nearby places: a beach, a place to eat, a viewpoint. Morning light.
9. Ten 5-second "still video" clips: curtains, water, palms, steam from coffee.
10. Night: lit pool, dinner table, lanterns.
